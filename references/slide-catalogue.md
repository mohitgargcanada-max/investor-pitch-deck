# Slide catalogue

*Part of the investor-pitch-deck skill, created by Mohit Garg.*

Pitch = in the 12–15 slide investor deck. Full = only in the long deck. Order below is the pitch order;
full-only slides are listed after.

## Pitch slides (in order)

| # | Slide | Headline pattern | Content | Evidence it needs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cover | Company name | One plain sentence with a number (who it is for, what they get); founder name, email, date. Logo here. | — |
| 2 | At a glance | The strongest proof as a sentence | 3–4 stat cards (lead with delivered output, not market size); one-line ask. | Company report date; official stat source |
| 3 | Problem | Who hurts and how | 2–3 pains, one cost-of-inaction number, ideally one customer quote. | Official stat or customer evidence |
| 4 | Solution / approach | The workflow change | Flow diagram (input → draft → confirm → act) or a product screenshot with before/after. | Product itself |
| 5 | Why now | The catalyst as a claim | What changed that makes this possible now (cost, regulation, adoption) + a sourced chart. Prefer the catalyst specific to you over generic "AI adoption is rising". | Official statistics, dated |
| 6 | Proof / traction | "N customers live, X delivered" | 3–4 big numbers with measurement date; the honest gap and the plan to close it. | Company data, dated |
| 7 | Market size | "Sized from the bottom up" | TAM/SAM/SOM = count of target businesses × blended price; assumptions stated; analyst figure only as cross-check. | Official business counts; your price list |
| 8 | Beachhead | Where we start | First segment + region, why it is the right wedge, data already in place. | Official counts by industry/region |
| 9 | Competition | What sets us apart | 2×2 map (two axes that matter) with named competitors, plus 3 "where we win" bullets. Never "no competitors". | Vendor sites, dated |
| 10 | Business model | Free to start, then … | Plans with prices (currency!) and what scales; cost-to-serve line. | Product pricing config |
| 11 | Go-to-market | The funnel | Phases (region/segment) + direct and partner channels. | Channel facts in full deck |
| 12 | Roadmap | Milestones to scale | 3–4 milestones with timing; what is live vs in build. | Product records |
| 13 | Team | Why this team wins | Names, photos, roles, 2-line bios on founder-market fit, LinkedIn. Never invent. | Founder |
| 14 | The ask | "[amount] pre-seed" | Amount, instrument, valuation/cap, runway; use of funds split; milestones it buys by when. | Founder + budget |
| 15 | Close | The memorable line | Plain sentence, contact, website. Logo here. | — |

## Optional slide library (full deck)

An optional slide goes in **only when its condition is met with verified facts** (see SKILL.md,
"Slide counts follow the evidence"). Skipped slides are listed for the user with the missing evidence.
Placement: "pitch" means it may replace or join a pitch slide; "appendix" means full deck only, after the
ask. Sources are what investors and VC firms recommend; check the deck's own stage before adding.

### Product and proof

| Slide | Include only if | Evidence it needs | Placement |
|---|---|---|---|
| How it works (step detail) | The product has a multi-step workflow worth showing | Real product steps | Appendix |
| Product demo / screenshots | A working product exists (not a mock-up passed off as real) | Real UI captures; label mock-ups as such. Don't open the deck with it | Pitch (solution) or appendix |
| Traction chart | At least 3 data points of a real metric over time | Dated metric series | Pitch (proof) or appendix |
| LOIs / pipeline / waitlist | Pre-revenue, but with signed or dated demand | Signed LOIs (price, trigger, signer), named pipeline, waitlist count and conversion | Pitch (replaces revenue traction) |
| Customer logos and quotes | Real customers or pilots who agreed to be named | Permission; quotes from real messages or calls | Appendix (or pitch proof) |
| Customer case study | 1–3 customers drive most of the traction | Before/after numbers, permission to name | Appendix |
| Milestones achieved timeline | The company has shipped dated milestones | Dated wins to date (not future plans) | Appendix |
| Cohort retention | 6+ months of customer or revenue cohorts | Cohort table: active at months 1/3/6/12 | Seed: appendix; Series A: pitch |
| KPI / usage dashboard | Series A or a diligence-heavy round | Growth, acquisition, engagement, retention on one page | Appendix / data room |

### Market and competition

| Slide | Include only if | Evidence it needs | Placement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Local competitor detail | Named competitors found in the target geography | Vendor names, prices, fair gaps | Appendix |
| Capability comparison table | At least 3 capabilities where you clearly differ | Verified vendor capabilities | Appendix |
| Look-alike tools worldwide | Comparable tools exist outside the target market | Vendor pricing pages, dated | Appendix |
| Gaps we can fill | The competitor research shows recurring, evidenced gaps | Prices/targets from the research | Appendix |
| Go-to-market by segment or region | The rollout differs meaningfully across segments or regions | Segment/region plan | Appendix |

### Business model and economics

| Slide | Include only if | Evidence it needs | Placement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unit economics (CAC, LTV, payback, gross margin) | There are paying customers (LTV must be profit-based) | Real acquisition cost, margin, churn | Seed: appendix or inside business model; Series A: pitch |
| Detailed business model | The pitch slide cannot hold the revenue logic | Pricing and revenue mechanics | Appendix |
| Per-channel CAC / sales funnel | Go-to-market has run, not only planned | Cost and conversion by channel | Appendix (Series A: pitch) |
| Burn multiple / efficiency | Series A with revenue history | Burn and net new ARR | Pitch (Series A) |
| Revenue model on today's price list | Real price list exists | Plan prices; counts labelled illustrative | Appendix |
| Financial projections | The founder supplies or approves the numbers | 12–18 month budget (preferred over 3–5 year projections) | Appendix |
| Marketing channels | Channel facts verified for the target region | Reach and cost facts per channel | Appendix |

### Product expansion

| Slide | Include only if | Evidence it needs | Placement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product / agent toolkit | The product has several live or in-build modules | Status per module from the product itself | Appendix |
| Expansion direction | Adjacent products have market price evidence | Vendor prices, demand data | Appendix |
| Products ranked by revenue potential | At least 4 candidate products researched | Verified prices and fit | Appendix |
| Detailed product roadmap | Product-focused investors, or the roadmap supports a business claim | Dated plan | Appendix |

### Moat, technology and AI

| Slide | Include only if | Evidence it needs | Placement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Technology and moat | A real mechanism exists (proprietary data, workflow lock-in, cost advantage) | What is proprietary vs off-the-shelf | Appendix (pitch only if it backs a claim) |
| Moat / flywheel / network effects | The flywheel is measurable, not aspirational | Metric that improves with scale (cost per unit falling, engagement rising) | Pitch or appendix |
| Technical architecture | Technical investors, or architecture is the differentiator | One diagram: proprietary vs bought | Appendix |
| AI cost to serve and margin path | AI-native product | Inference/review cost per unit, gross margin now and trend | Pitch or appendix |
| AI accuracy / evaluation evidence | AI-native product with measured quality | Versioned eval results tied to what customers care about | Appendix |
| Data rights and model dependency | AI-native product | Data provenance, training rights in contracts, model licence, fallback if the vendor changes terms | Appendix |
| IP / patent strategy | Patents filed or a genuine IP differentiator | Filings, claims | Appendix |
| Security and compliance posture | Selling to enterprises or regulated buyers | Certification status (say "in progress" if so), controls | Appendix |

### Sector-specific

| Slide | Include only if | Evidence it needs | Placement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Marketplace liquidity | Marketplaces only | Match rate, sell-through, repeat rate, take rate; show before GMV | Pitch |
| GMV retention cohorts | Marketplaces with 12+ months of data | Supply and demand cohorts | Pitch (Series A) |
| Regulatory approval path | Health, biotech, fintech, energy, defence | Regulator, status, dated milestones, who pays | Pitch (health) or appendix |
| Hardware BOM and manufacturing | Hardware only | Line-item BOM, margin now vs at volume, certification | Pitch or appendix |
| Impact / theory of change | Pitching impact investors | Logic model with recognised impact metrics | Pitch (impact funds only) |

### Company, financing and diligence

| Slide | Include only if | Evidence it needs | Placement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Founder story / purpose | Always worth considering; essential at pre-seed | The founders' real reason and insight; no inflated credentials | Pitch (early) |
| Vision / end state | Pre-seed and seed | Where the company is in 5–10 years, grounded in today's product | Pitch (near the end) |
| Use of funds | The ask amount is known | Split by category in currency, tied to milestones | Pitch (inside the ask) or appendix |
| What the funding changes | Concrete before/after exists | Today vs with funding, per area | Appendix |
| Hiring plan | Asked how money is spent, or Series A | Role-by-role plan by quarter | Seed: appendix; Series A: team slide |
| Advisors / board / investors | Named people who agreed to be listed | Permission | Inside team or proof |
| Cap table and prior funding | Series A, or an investor asks | Ownership, option pool, SAFE conversions | Appendix / data room |
| Risks and mitigation / strengths, weaknesses and fixes | High-risk market, or the founder wants the gaps stated | Real gaps with specific fixes | Appendix |
| Questions we keep getting | The same question recurs in investor meetings | Short, direct answers | Appendix |
| Internal recommendations | Never sent to investors | — | Last slide of the full deck, marked "delete before sending" |

### Leave out by default

- **Exit / comparables slide** before a growth round: VCs read it as narrow thinking and cash-out intent
  ("companies don't get sold, they get bought" — Hunter Walk). Answer verbally if asked.
- **3–5 year detailed projections** at pre-seed/seed: a 12–18 month milestone plan carries more weight.
- **Runway-only milestones** ("18–24 months"): state what the money achieves, then the timeline.
- **Press/awards, customer-journey, persona and standalone market-map slides**: no investor source
  recommends them; fold anything useful into proof or competition.

## Writing rules per slide

- Headline = the claim; kicker (small caps above) = the topic.
- One message per slide; if a slide needs two headlines it is two slides.
- Body text ≥ 13–14 pt, captions 9–10 pt, titles 32 pt.
- Every number has a date and a source on the same slide.
- Money always carries its currency; analyst figures keep their own currency (US$) even in a CAD deck.
- Speaker notes: 2–4 sentences of what to say, including the honest caveat.
